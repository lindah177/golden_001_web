Who We Are

A Safe Haven for Children in Need

Golden Ark Care Centre has been a beacon of hope since 2010. We provide a nurturing home, quality education, and emotional support to vulnerable children — ensuring they grow up with dignity, love, and the tools to thrive.

Our team of dedicated caregivers, educators, and volunteers work tirelessly to create a world where no child is left behind.
